## What Didn't Go Well

- **The entire Inception phase was skipped.** No intent was written, no elaboration session was run, no units were decomposed, and no bolt was planned. Work jumped straight from archaeology findings to code generation. The mob elaboration turn structure, the design session, unit sign-off, and the bolt risk assessment all have no artifacts.
- **No bolt file was created before work began.** The bolt risk assessment was skipped entirely. Work began directly from archaeology findings without a signed-off risk assessment.
- **EC-011 was a false positive from the archaeology phase.** The archaeology agent confused `DateOnly.DayNumber` (absolute epoch-based day count) with `DayOfYear` (day within the current year). The finding lived undetected in `edge-cases.md` and was carried forward into the AC proposals without being challenged.
- **A breaking API contract change was not identified before execution.** Adding `BaseRate` to `CreateCabinRequest` changed the shape of `POST /api/cabins`. This broke 8 existing test call sites and requires any existing API clients to update their payloads. The contract-change form of the default AC was not invoked and no Breaking Changes Register was produced.
- **Engineer accepted all ACs without individual review.** The 5 proposed ACs were approved in a single response ("these are fine") without any challenge, modification, or stress-testing. This allowed the EC-011 false positive to pass through into the work.
